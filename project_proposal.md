Description: One of the factors contributing to traffic congestion is the use of fixed-time traffic light cycles, which do not adapt to changes in traffic volume. By AI to dynamically adjust traffic light timing based on real-time traffic conditions, we may be able to reduce traffic congestion and shorten vehicle waiting and travel times


Scope: Develop two systems and compare traffic behaviors
One is a traditional traffic light system that changes signals at predefined time intervals
Another uses AI to dynamically adjust signal timing based on traffic conditions


M&S goals:
Develop a reinforcement learning based signal controller to optimize signal timing
At least two traffic control systems using the same road intersection and vehicle generation model 
Compare the performance of both systems, such as the number of waiting vehicles, individual vehicle waiting times, average waiting time, etc.
